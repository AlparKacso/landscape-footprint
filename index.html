Start with the Extract Wizard →
Where to click. Press Load an extract → Use the extract shipped with this prototype, step through the four wizard steps, then open the Landscape Footprint from the left rail. The one control worth playing with is the transition path across the top of Position — the calls themselves change, not just the ordering.

The written deliverables

1 · Increment Breakdown Now / Next / Later, and what is custom, product or standalone. 2 · Deployment Design The first four weeks with the real customer — and what would stop it. 3 · Build Log What was built, what failed, what was cut, and where the hours went. 4 · Handbook A non-technical walkthrough of the model, the evidence and the decisions.
What is not here. The candidate brief and the screenshots of the Celonis tenant used to sample the design tokens are deliberately not published — the tokens themselves live in prototype/app.css, which is the only part of them that needed to survive. The four CSVs are the synthetic, anonymised extract provided with the challenge.
